Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

ACS Field Tools is a work tool for technicians and contractors of Automation & Control Systems LLC ("ACS"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What the app sends to ACS

When a technician finishes a project, the app uploads the project's reports to the ACS quality system. A report contains:

  • the technician's name as typed into the app
  • the project, panel and work order the report belongs to
  • the equipment checked (model, serial number, network address) and the result for each I/O point or drive setting

To show the list of open projects, the app also asks the ACS quality system for that list. Both need an access key that ACS issues to its technicians.

ACS uses this information only for its quality records and keeps it under its normal business record retention.

What stays on the device

Reports that have not been sent yet, the technician's name, the access key and app settings are stored on the device. They are removed when the app is deleted.

Photos and the optional AI assistant

The Drive Setup tool has an optional page that reads a wiring schematic or motor nameplate from photos the technician takes or picks. Those photos are sent to an AI service only when the technician has entered an AI service key or an ACS AI server address and taps Analyze. With the technician's own key, the photos go to Anthropic's Claude API under Anthropic's terms. The app does not use the camera or photo library for anything else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking
  • No location, contacts or other personal data from the device
  • No sharing or selling of information to third parties, other than the AI service described above when the technician chooses to use it

Local network

The app talks to industrial controllers, I/O racks and drives on the wired network the device is plugged into. That traffic stays on the local network.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
